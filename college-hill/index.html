Tree-lined streets and the drains beneath them in College Hill

College Hill grew up alongside Washburn University starting in the 1880s, and its early boosters planted trees so widely that elms, hackberries and catalpas from that era still shade some blocks. That canopy sits above sewer laterals that may be nearly as old as the houses they serve. Root growth and aging joints are a common pairing in a neighborhood like this.

A neighborhood planted for shade

Washburn's leaders pushed tree planting across College Hill from its earliest years, and some trees dating to the 1880s are still standing. A mature root system can spread well past the edge of the canopy. Any older lateral within reach is a possible target.

Many styles of house, several generations of pipe

College Hill's homes range from Queen Anne and Folk Victorian houses to Craftsman bungalows and later ranches. That mix means neighboring homes can have completely different pipe materials underground. What worked on the house next door says little about your line.

When a clog keeps coming back

In a heavily treed neighborhood, a recurring main-line clog often points to roots finding the same opening again. Clearing the line treats the symptom. A camera check can show whether a joint needs repair to break the cycle.

Questions about drains in College Hill

Does an older tree near my yard mean roots in my sewer line?

Not necessarily, but the odds rise when mature trees sit near an aging lateral. An inspection can confirm whether roots are present before anyone assumes they are the cause.

Homes here vary a lot in age. Does that change the cleaning approach?

Yes. Pipe material and condition drive the decision, and both vary widely in this neighborhood. The assessment should be specific to your line.

Is hydro jetting a one-time fix for root problems?

It can remove existing growth from a sound pipe, but roots return if the entry point remains. Recurring root intrusions may call for repair rather than repeated cleaning.

Tell us what is happening with your drains.

Whether the issue is a kitchen line that keeps clogging or a main sewer backup, describe the symptoms and how long they have been going on. A drain professional can determine whether hydro jetting is suitable after reviewing the line.
