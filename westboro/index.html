Longer yards, longer laterals

On a deep lot, the private service lateral may run a considerable distance before it reaches the public main. A blockage near the far end can produce the same slow drains as one near the foundation. Locating the obstruction matters before choosing how to clear it.

Established landscaping and buried lines

Decades of mature trees and shrubs shade Westboro's yards. Root systems that old have had a long time to find any weakness in a buried joint. If clogs recur, ask whether roots are entering the line and where.

Questions about drains in Westboro

Does a longer sewer lateral clog more easily?

Length alone does not cause clogs, but it gives buildup and roots more territory to affect. It also makes locating a blockage more important, since symptoms at the house can start far down the line.

What pipe materials are common in 1920s and 1930s homes?

Homes of that era often used cast iron indoors and clay tile for buried laterals, though many lines have been repaired or replaced since. An inspection is the reliable way to learn what your home has now.

Will cleaning fix a recurring clog for good?

It can if the pipe is sound and the cause is removed. If roots keep entering through the same opening or the pipe wall is damaged, the clog will tend to return until that is addressed.

Tell us what is happening with your drains.

Whether the issue is a kitchen line that keeps clogging or a main sewer backup, describe the symptoms and how long they have been going on. A drain professional can determine whether hydro jetting is suitable after reviewing the line.
