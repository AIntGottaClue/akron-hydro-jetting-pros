What is happening inside the line?

Grease does not stay liquid all the way down the line. It cools against the pipe wall, traps scraps and soap, then narrows the path for wastewater. In Topeka, the city lists grease and food waste among the materials that contribute to sewer blockages.

How hydro jetting may help

High-pressure water can work around the inside of a suitable pipe rather than only cutting a path through the center of a clog. Equipment may run up to 4,000 PSI, but the setting and nozzle must match the pipe condition. A camera inspection can help locate the buildup and flag cracks or weak sections before cleaning.

When to ask for a closer look

A backed-up kitchen sink, gurgling nearby fixtures or another clog soon after snaking are reasons to ask about the main line. Grease collected in a damaged pipe may return unless the defect is repaired. Keeping cooking fat out of drains is still the best way to reduce the next blockage.

Questions about this drain problem

Can hydro jetting clear a grease-clogged kitchen line?

It can clear grease in a suitable line, but an inspection should determine whether the blockage is in a branch drain or the main sewer line. The pipe condition guides the choice of equipment and pressure.

Why does my kitchen drain clog again after it was cleared?

A cable may open a path through the obstruction without removing grease along the pipe wall. A professional can check whether residue remains and whether the line is sound enough for another cleaning method.

Should I pour grease down a Topeka drain with hot water?

No. The City of Topeka advises letting cooking grease cool and putting it in the trash. Hot water may move grease farther down the line, where it can collect again.
