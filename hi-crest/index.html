Postwar homes and mid-century drain lines in Hi-Crest

Hi-Crest took shape after World War II, when it was developed for military personnel stationed at Forbes Field. Its postwar houses are newer than Topeka's streetcar-era neighborhoods, but their drain lines have still been in the ground for seventy years or more. Mid-century materials age in their own ways.

Built quickly for a growing air base

Hi-Crest's homes went up fast in the years after the war to house Forbes Field families. Construction of that era used the standard pipe materials of the day, including cast iron indoors and clay tile for many buried laterals. Decades later, those lines are well into old age.

Seventy years of kitchens

Grease and sludge accumulate slowly, and a postwar kitchen line may have served several generations of cooking. A line that drains again after snaking can still carry heavy buildup along its walls. A thorough cleaning addresses the layer that a quick clearing leaves behind.

Small homes, same big questions

The right first step here is the same as in any Topeka neighborhood: find out what is in the line and what shape the pipe is in. Age alone does not condemn a mid-century lateral, but it earns a closer look before high-pressure cleaning.

Questions about drains in Hi-Crest

Are postwar pipes better than prewar ones?

They are newer, but a line from the late 1940s or 1950s has still been in service for many decades. Condition matters more than the decade, and an inspection shows condition directly.

Why does my kitchen sink keep slowing down?

Years of grease and food residue can coat the inside of a kitchen line even when water still gets through. A thorough cleaning can remove that buildup when the pipe itself is sound.

Does Hi-Crest have tree root problems too?

Any neighborhood with established trees and aging buried lines can. The area's postwar trees have had decades to grow, so roots are worth checking when clogs recur.

Tell us what is happening with your drains.

Whether the issue is a kitchen line that keeps clogging or a main sewer backup, describe the symptoms and how long they have been going on. A drain professional can determine whether hydro jetting is suitable after reviewing the line.
